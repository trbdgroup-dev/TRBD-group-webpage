# Content guide

How to edit the TRBD website. Everything a visitor sees, every word and every picture, can be changed in the CMS without a developer.

## Signing in

Go to **`/cms`** on the website (for example `https://www.trbdgroup.com/cms`) and sign in with the email and password you were given. If you've forgotten your password, ask the site's super admin to reset it.

There are two kinds of account:

- **Content editor:** edits and publishes pages, verticals, images, site settings and navigation.
- **Super admin:** everything a content editor can do, plus managing staff accounts.

## What's where

The CMS dashboard has three groups:

| Group    | Item              | What it controls                                                                                                       |
| -------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Content  | **Pages**         | The home page (address `home`), the privacy notice and any new pages                                                   |
|          | **Verticals**     | The five verticals: their pages, the hero tiles and slides on the home page, the "What we do" rows and the footer list |
|          | **Media**         | Every image on the site                                                                                                |
| Admin    | **Users**         | Staff accounts (super admins only)                                                                                     |
| Settings | **Site settings** | Company name and contact details, social links, the default link-preview image, search engine codes                    |
|          | **Navigation**    | The header links, the header button and the footer's "Company" links                                                   |

## Drafts, publishing and preview

Pages and verticals save automatically as you type. **Saving is not publishing:** visitors only see your changes after you click **Publish changes**.

- **Live Preview** (top right of the edit screen) shows the real page beside the form, updated a moment after you type. Use the phone, tablet and desktop sizes to check each layout.
- **Publish changes** puts your version live. The website updates within seconds; there is nothing else to do.
- **Schedule** (the arrow next to Publish) publishes at a date and time you choose.
- **Versions** lists every earlier version. You can compare them and restore an old one.
- **Unpublish** takes a page off the site without deleting it.

Clicking **Create new** saves an empty draft straight away, because of autosave. If you change your mind, delete that draft; empty drafts are never shown on the site.

When you're previewing a page outside the CMS, a dark bar at the bottom says "Preview: showing unpublished drafts". Click **Exit preview** to see the site as visitors do.

## First job: review the vertical copy

Each vertical has two layers:

1. **What's live now:** the wording from the original website (summary, who we work with, four one-line services) and the hero headline.
2. **A draft waiting for you:** a paragraph for each service, a "How we deliver" section and 3–4 common questions. This was drafted from the original website's wording only. It adds no facts, figures, client names, certifications or licences.

For each vertical:

1. Open **Verticals** and choose the vertical. Its **Copy status** says "Draft – needs review".
2. Open **Live Preview** and read the page, including the questions at the bottom.
3. Edit anything that isn't accurate or doesn't sound like TRBD. Add facts only the firm can confirm, such as licences, certifications, sectors served or countries.
4. Check the **hero headline** on the Overview tab: it appears on the home page slideshow.
5. Set **Copy status** to "Reviewed" and click **Publish changes**.

The "How we deliver" and "Common questions" sections only appear on the live site once a vertical with them is published. If a vertical has none, those sections are simply left out.

## Editing pages

A page is a list of **sections** (blocks). In **Pages → Home**:

- Click a section to expand and edit it.
- Drag the handle to reorder sections.
- Use **Add Layout** to add a section, and the section's menu to remove it.
- The **Hero** section's photo and title are the opening view. The five vertical tiles come from Verticals automatically.
- The **Verticals list** section shows a row for every vertical automatically; you only edit its heading and intro.
- **Anchor** on a section (for example "How we work") lets header and footer links jump to it.

Section types you'll mainly use: Hero, Section intro, Verticals list, Approach stages, Why points, Image and text, Call to action, Contact and Rich text.

**Stats** and **Testimonials** exist for later. Only use them with figures the firm can stand behind and quotes clients have approved in writing.

### Adding a new page

**Pages → Create new**, give it a title and an address (for example `about-us`: lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens). Some addresses are reserved by the site (such as `cms`, `api`, `preview` and the five vertical addresses); the CMS will tell you if you pick one. Add sections, publish, then add a link to it in **Navigation** if it should appear in the header or footer.

## Images

- **Alt text is required.** Describe what the picture shows for people who can't see it, for example "Rows of solar panels on a green field". Don't start with "Image of".
- **Credit and source:** the current photos are free stock photos from Unsplash. Their credit and source link are filled in. Leave credit empty for the firm's own photos.
- **Focal point:** after uploading, click the part of the picture that must never be cropped, such as a face. The site keeps that point in view on every screen size.
- **Replacing a stock photo:** upload the new image in **Media**, then choose it in the vertical's **Hero image** (or the home page Hero's background). Don't present people in stock photos as TRBD staff or clients.
- Use landscape photos at least 2000 pixels wide for heroes and verticals. JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF; up to 20 MB.

## Search engines and link previews

Every page and vertical has an **SEO** tab. All of it is optional. When it's empty, the site uses the page title, its summary or first paragraph, and its main photo.

- **Meta title:** shown in Google results and browser tabs (about 50–60 characters). " | TRBD Consulting Group" is added automatically, except on the home page.
- **Meta description:** the line under the title in Google (about 120–155 characters).
- **Link preview image:** what shows when the page is shared on WhatsApp, LinkedIn or X. Without one, the page's own photo or the default from Site Settings is used, or a branded image is generated.
- **Hide from search engines:** keeps the page out of Google and the sitemap.

In **Site settings → SEO**, paste the **Google Search Console** and **Bing** verification codes when you set those services up (just the code, not the whole tag).

## Site settings and navigation

- **Site settings → Contact:** fill in the phone number, office address and working hours. While they're empty, they're simply not shown. The email address appears on the Contact section, every "Email us" button and the error pages.
- **Navigation:** header links (up to six), the header button (leave it empty to keep "Contact us") and the footer's Company links. A link can point to a page, a vertical, a section of the home page or another website.

## Each vertical's team inbox

On each vertical, **Team inbox** (right-hand column) is where that vertical's enquiries will be sent once the enquiry form is added in stage 2. It's never shown on the website. Fill it in before stage 2 goes live.
