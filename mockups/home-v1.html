Who we are

One partner, one point of accountability.

TRBD Consulting Group Private Limited helps organisations hire the right workforce, build their skills, run energy projects, move to cleaner power and grow across borders. A single account lead coordinates every part of your project, so you work with one team instead of several vendors.

What we do

Five verticals, connected by one team.

Our verticals often work together. A new energy project may need skilled crews, safety training and market-entry support at the same time, and we can deliver all three.

How we work

From first brief to long-term support

Every engagement follows the same four stages, whether we are filling fifty positions or opening a new market.

Understand

We study your goals, site conditions, compliance needs and timelines before proposing anything.

Plan

You get a clear scope, schedule and cost, with named owners for each deliverable.

Deliver

Our team mobilises people, programmes or projects and reports progress against the plan.

Sustain

We stay on for follow-up, audits, retraining and replacement so results hold over time.
